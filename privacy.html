Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information PlayNova (“we”, “us”) collects when you use our website and how we use it. By using the site you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Information stored on your device: your player name, scores, favourites, recently played games and preferences are saved in your browser’s local storage. We do not require an account.
  • Information you send us: if you use the contact form or join the Premium waiting list, we receive the details you enter (name, email, message).
  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider may log your IP address, browser type and pages visited for security and analytics.

2. Cookies and advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your choices and to show advertising. Third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdSense) may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your Google Ads settings or via aboutads.info. You can also clear cookies and local storage from your browser at any time.

3. How we use information

  • To run the site, save your progress and display leaderboards.
  • To respond to your messages.
  • To improve our games and prevent abuse.
  • To show advertising that keeps the site free.

4. Children’s privacy

PlayNova is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers needed to run the site (hosting, analytics, advertising) and when required by law. We use HTTPS and industry-standard safeguards, but no online service is perfectly secure.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Contact us to make a request.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
