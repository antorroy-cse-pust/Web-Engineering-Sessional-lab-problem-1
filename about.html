About PlayNova

PlayNova is a free online gaming portal where anyone can jump into a game in seconds – no downloads, no installs and no account needed. Every game runs in your browser on desktop, tablet and mobile.

Our mission

We believe great casual games should be instantly accessible. We curate quick, fun and family-friendly games across six categories: Puzzle, Action, Racing, Arcade, Adventure and Brain Training.

Services at a glance

  • Instant play – HTML5 games that load fast and work on any modern device.
  • Leaderboards – compete for the top score in every game.
  • Favourites & history – pick up where you left off.
  • Premium (coming soon) – an optional ad-free membership.
  • For developers – we welcome legally licensed HTML5 games. Get in touch.

How PlayNova is funded

PlayNova is free to play and supported by non-intrusive advertising. We only list games from creators who have given permission or released their work under an open licence.
